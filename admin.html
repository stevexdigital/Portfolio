Notifications

Kept private — only you can see this address, and notifications are sent by your database, not visitors' browsers.

Every notification includes X-Webhook-Timestamp and X-Webhook-Signature headers. To confirm a request really came from your site, compute HMAC-SHA256 of timestamp + "." + raw body with this secret and compare it to the signature (after sha256=). Reject requests older than 5 minutes. The Test button above only checks the address is reachable and isn't signed.
